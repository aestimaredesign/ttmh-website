The Origin

It started as a pure visual experiment — a single strong shape that reads clearly even when reduced. The raised hand made people pause and comment, confirming the gesture was doing the work.

From there, it expanded into a full character with different expressions, hair, and accessories while keeping the same pose language. The hand is both a barrier and an invitation. Each piece is a different mood, but the character stays consistent.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Talk To My Hand?
Talk To My Hand is a character-driven 10,000-piece PFP collection built around the gesture of a raised hand. It is also an evolving ecosystem that includes creative tools (TTMH Studio) and future utility for holders.
When is the mint and what is the supply?
The official collection launch is planned for December. The supply is strictly limited to 10,000 pieces. Exact date and mint price will be announced soon.
Which blockchain will TTMH be on?
The collection will be deployed on the Robinhood Chain (an EVM-compatible network). You will need a compatible wallet (like MetaMask or Robinhood Wallet) to mint.
What is the Soft Staking Utility?
We are exploring a low-maintenance mechanism where holders can potentially lock their NFTs or hold them in their wallet to receive future perks. NFT locking and simple holding are different mechanics, and we are exploring both without committing to complex or heavy tokenomics.
